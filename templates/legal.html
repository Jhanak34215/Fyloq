Important legal summary

Fyloq provides temporary technical infrastructure for file sharing. Files are uploaded by users, and users remain responsible for ensuring that their content and use of the service are lawful.

Fyloq is a temporary file-sharing platform developed and operated from India.

02

Purpose of the Service

Fyloq enables users to upload temporary files and retrieve them using a generated access code and optional PIN.

The service may provide:

  • Temporary file storage.
  • Access-code based retrieval.
  • Optional PIN protection.
  • One-time download settings.
  • Automatic file expiry and deletion.
  • Support and abuse-reporting facilities.

Fyloq is not intended to function as permanent cloud storage, archival storage or a guaranteed backup service.

03

User-Uploaded Content

Fyloq does not create, select or own files uploaded by users. The uploader remains responsible for the content, legality, accuracy and sharing of every uploaded file.

  • Uploading a file does not transfer ownership to Fyloq.
  • The uploader must have permission to store and share the file.
  • The uploader is responsible for protecting the access code and PIN.
  • Anyone receiving valid credentials may be able to access the file.
  • Fyloq does not manually inspect every uploaded file.
04

Prohibited and Unlawful Content

Fyloq must not be used to store or share illegal, harmful or unauthorized material.

  • Malware, ransomware, spyware or computer viruses.
  • Copyrighted files shared without authorization.
  • Stolen or unlawfully obtained information.
  • Fraud, phishing or impersonation material.
  • Private personal data shared without lawful authority.
  • Threatening, harassing, blackmailing or abusive content.
  • Child sexual abuse material or illegal sexual content.
  • Terrorism-related or unlawful extremist material.
  • Content prohibited by applicable laws or valid court orders.
Zero-tolerance notice

Files reasonably suspected of containing illegal or harmful material may be disabled or deleted without prior notice.

05

Legal, Copyright and Abuse Complaints

A person who believes that a Fyloq file violates a legal right may submit a complaint through the Report Abuse page or support email.

The complaint should include:

  • Full name and valid contact email.
  • Relevant Fyloq access code.
  • Description of the file or activity being reported.
  • The legal right or policy believed to be violated.
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization where relevant.
  • A statement confirming that the submitted information is accurate.

Incomplete, false, abusive or misleading complaints may not be processed.

06

Review, Restriction and Content Removal

Fyloq may restrict, disable or permanently delete a file where reasonably necessary.

Reasons may include:

  • A valid legal or copyright complaint.
  • Suspected malware or cybersecurity threats.
  • Violation of the Terms & Conditions.
  • Risk to users, infrastructure or the public.
  • A lawful court, government or regulatory request.
  • Expiry or completion of a one-time download.

Removal may occur without notifying the uploader where notice is impossible, unsafe or prohibited by law.

Fyloq may collect and review security logs, failed access attempts and technical information needed to protect the service.

Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported responsibly and must not be exploited, published or used to access another user's file.

Serious security incidents may be handled or reported in accordance with applicable legal and cybersecurity obligations.
