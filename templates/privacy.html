Privacy summary

Fyloq is designed for temporary file transfer. Files may be deleted automatically after expiry or after a one-time download. Users should not use Fyloq as permanent backup storage.

01

Introduction

Fyloq provides temporary file-upload, access-code and download services. This Privacy Policy applies when you use the Fyloq website, submit a support request, report abuse or interact with related services.

By using Fyloq, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable law.

02

Information We Process

Depending on how you use Fyloq, we may process:

📄

Uploaded file information

Original filename, file extension, file size, generated storage name, upload time, expiry time, access code, download count and file status.

🔐

Security information

PIN hashes, failed PIN attempts, temporary lock information and short-lived download tokens.

🎧

Support information

Name, email address, optional access code, problem type, subject, message, ticket ID, submission time and request status.

⚙️

Technical information

Server logs, request timestamps, browser-generated information, error details and security events necessary to operate and protect the service.

03

Uploaded Files and Temporary Storage

Files uploaded to Fyloq are stored only for the selected availability period or until a one-time download is completed, depending on the settings selected by the uploader.

  • Files are assigned a randomly generated stored filename.
  • The original filename may be stored to provide the correct download name.
  • A six-digit access code is generated for file retrieval.
  • An optional PIN is stored only as a one-way password hash.
  • Files may be removed automatically after expiry.
  • One-time files may be removed after the first successful download.
  • Fyloq should not be treated as permanent cloud backup.
Important

Fyloq cannot display or recover an uploader's original PIN because the PIN is not stored in readable form.

04

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Upload, store, retrieve and delete temporary files.
  • Generate access codes, QR codes and temporary download links.
  • Verify PINs and protect files from unauthorized access.
  • Detect expired, missing, downloaded or unavailable files.
  • Respond to support requests and abuse complaints.
  • Maintain service security, reliability and performance.
  • Investigate suspected misuse, malware, fraud or illegal activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and valid government requests.
05

Data Retention

Fyloq aims to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Information General Retention Approach
Uploaded files Until selected expiry, one-time download or administrative removal.
File database records May remain for operational, security, audit or statistical purposes after the file itself is removed.
Support requests Retained while the request is being handled and for a reasonable period afterward for support history and dispute management.
Security and technical logs Retained for a limited period based on security, troubleshooting and legal requirements.

Information may be retained for a longer period when required by law, necessary to investigate abuse or required to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

06

Security

Fyloq uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect files and personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

  • Randomized stored filenames.
  • One-way hashing for optional file PINs.
  • Temporary signed download links.
  • Wrong-PIN attempt limits and temporary locking.
  • File-size and allowed-extension restrictions.
  • Administrative access controls.
  • Automatic expiry checks and cleanup.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting access codes, PINs, devices and downloaded files.

07

Information Sharing

Fyloq does not sell users' personal information. Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary, including:

  • To infrastructure, hosting, database, email or security service providers.
  • To investigate abuse, malware, fraud or threats to users and systems.
  • To comply with applicable law, court orders or valid governmental requests.
  • To protect the rights, property, security and integrity of Fyloq and its users.
  • During a merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of the service, subject to applicable safeguards.
08

Cookies and Session Data

Fyloq may use essential session cookies required for secure administrator login and basic website operation. These cookies are not intended for advertising.

Future versions may use privacy-conscious analytics or additional cookies. If that occurs, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

09

User Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law and the information available to identify a request, users may contact Fyloq to:

  • Ask what personal information was provided through a support request.
  • Request correction of inaccurate support information.
  • Request deletion of eligible support information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a privacy complaint or grievance.

Fyloq may request reasonable information to verify the requester and prevent unauthorized access or deletion. A file access code alone may not establish ownership of a file.

10

Children's Privacy

Fyloq is not specifically designed for children. A child should use the service only with the involvement and permission of a parent or lawful guardian where required by applicable law.

Users must not knowingly upload children's sensitive or personal information without a lawful reason and appropriate authorization.

11

Fyloq infrastructure or service providers may process information in India or in other locations where hosting, storage, security or support services operate.

Where required, appropriate measures will be considered for international processing and transfers under applicable law.

The effective date and version displayed at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published. Continued use after an update indicates acknowledgment of the revised policy, where permitted by law.
